The Golden Rules of Swedish Sentence Structure
Rule 1: Basic Order of Subject → Verb → Object
Jag dricker kaffe.  |  Hon läser en bok.  |  Vi äter middag.
Rule 2: The V2 Rule: Verb always comes 2nd, no matter what starts the sentence.
Idag äter jag lunch.  |  Nu går vi hem.  |  Imorgon reser hon.
Rule 3: Keep It Short. Split long ideas into two sentences.
Eftersom det regnar ute så stannar jag hemma idag.
Det regnar ute. Jag stannar hemma.
Rule 4: Use "man" instead of passive voice to sound natural.
Man äter middag klockan sex.  |  Man säger inte så.

The Most Swedish Word (Probably)
lagom
not too much, not too little; just right
"Det är lagom varmt idag."
(It's just the right temperature today.)
Use lagom wherever it sounds good and it will impress many native speakers.
